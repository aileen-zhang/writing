## EXT street 
pigeons rise on the snap of their wings.  
wire stairs wind up to heaven.  
and in them, a-humming,  
the grumble of life, our mother of cycles:  
breath, step, enzyme, song—  
for whom return is revival.

I beg her to bless me:  
sing it again!
